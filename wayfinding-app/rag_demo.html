Welcome to the City of Calamba

Calamba, officially the City of Calamba, is a 1st class component city in the province of Laguna, Philippines. According to the 2020 census, it has a population of 539,671 people. Only 54 kilometers away from Metro Manila.

Paano kumuha ng business permit?

Para sa Application for Business Permit Renewal (On-Site / Walk-In), narito ang mga kailangan mong ihanda:

  1. Unified Application Form na may tamang kasagutan ↳ secure at: Calamba City website (calambacity.gov.ph) / BPTFO B.O.S.S. Area
  2. Katunayan ng Kabuuang Benta o Gross Receipts ↳ ikaw ang maghahanda nito (provided by you)
  3. Barangay Business Clearance ↳ secure at: Barangay Hall
  4. Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) ↳ secure at: City Treasury Management Office

Kapag kumpleto na ang mga ito, pumunta sa opisina sa ibaba para iproseso ang iyong permit.
